Paper run: the playbook basket, six months, real prices, pretend money

The one strategy from the search that looked worth a second look, run forward instead of backward. Every day just after 00:00 UTC, when the daily bar closes, the server fetches the day's Coinbase closes, runs the same four rules the backtest ran on the same 15 coins, and turns each changed signal into an order filled at Binance.US's live best bid or ask at that moment plus the 2 bp fee. $10,000, four rules with $2,500 each, no coin over 10% of a rule's money. Two things are being tested: whether real fills track the backtest's assumed fill at the bar's close, and whether the rules go on working after the data they were chosen on ended. Nothing here trades for real, and six months is short — see the note under the chart.

What each rule holds now

Each rule's $2,500 is its own account; the rules never move money between each other (the backtest re-balanced them daily, a small difference).

Every order

"Share" is the rule's account share in that coin before and after. Buys fill at the best ask, sells at the best bid, each plus 2 bp. A buy "short of cash" was scaled down to what the rule had, as the backtest does.
