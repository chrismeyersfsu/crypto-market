Strategy search: every idea tried, with real costs and a held-back part

A wider net than triangles: trend-following, short-term reversion, one exchange leading another, order flow, pairs of coins that move together, and timing by hour, day and volatility — each family run over a year or more of candles (Binance.US and Coinbase, 1-minute to daily; 100+ Binance.US markets hourly) and 30 days of every Binance.US trade. One harness scores them all: the rule decided at a bar's close is held over the next bar (so it can only use what it could have known), every change of position pays the fee plus half the bid–ask spread, nothing is sold that isn't held, and the last 30% of the bars is held back — parameters were chosen on the first 70% only, and the held-back part is what to believe. "Buy & hold" is what simply holding the coin returned over the same bars, the bar an idea has to clear.
